# Panoramic research map background

Generated using the built-in `image_gen` tool. The tool did not expose a model selector. Final artwork: [research-panorama.png](../src/assets/research-panorama.png).

This image is decorative terrain. All research routes, labels, paper flags and interactions are implemented separately in SVG from `data/catalog.json`.

## Prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: subtle panoramic background artwork for an interactive academic research map; NOT a complete UI, not a data chart.
Primary request: A wide, overhead comic-book adventure atlas landscape printed on warm ivory paper, to be used behind 37 clickable research milestone flags and 5 routes that will be drawn later in code. Panoramic ratio 3:1.
Composition: large continuous imaginary landscape, not separate panels, with 85 percent open pale paper and broad quiet clearings for labels. A few very faint irregular geographical regions in pale sky blue, soft coral, pale mint, butter yellow and lavender blend into the ivory field through fine ink boundaries. Small charming hand-inked contour hills and one mountain cluster on the extreme upper left border, a tiny sparse tree grove at the bottom left border, a tiny observatory outline at the extreme upper right border, subtle coastline-like contour curves near the lower right edge, and a few fine topographic contour loops near the edges. Keep all landmarks peripheral, small and low contrast.
Style: sophisticated whimsical indie science comic, hand-inked outlines, flat muted spot colors, light halftone print dots, very subtle paper grain. Restrained linework, harmonious pastel colors, no heavy dark areas, no visual clutter. Top-down cartographic view, not isometric, not perspective. No division into boxes.
Critical constraints: absolutely NO words, letters, numbers, legends, labels, titles, logos, flags, location pins, roads, paths, connectors or UI. Do not invent research content. These will all be implemented separately as real interactive elements. Avoid large foreground objects, dark forests, strong shadows, gradients, 3D rendering or populated cities. The central and most of the full image must remain near-white and readable underneath black text.
```
