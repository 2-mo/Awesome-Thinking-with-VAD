
## Illustration strip

`src/assets/idea-strip.png` is a generated decorative five-scene strip, used only as artwork. It is not evidence imagery from the catalog papers.

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: production illustration strip used as 5 tiny comic panel banners in an academic video anomaly understanding web application.
Create a wide horizontal contact sheet consisting of EXACTLY FIVE equally wide scenes in a single row, edge to edge, no gaps, no captions, no typography, no labels, no frames outside the art. Aspect ratio 3:1. Each scene takes exactly 20% of width. All scenes share one consistent sophisticated indie science comic style, crisp expressive black ink outlines, warm paper whites, sparse halftone dots, solid pastel spot colors, highly legible silhouettes, restrained textures.
Scene 1 background pale sky blue: a black-and-white film frame showing a walking pedestrian and a small red warning triangle, beside two interlocking speech and image symbols suggesting semantic alignment.
Scene 2 background pale coral: a clipboard with simple nontext checkmarks and a magnifying glass, representing explicit anomaly rules and learned questions.
Scene 3 background pale mint: a curious adult scientist in profile connecting three small evidence frames with pencil arrows, representing reasoning and fact verification.
Scene 4 background butter yellow: an adult detective examining a strip of video through a large magnifying glass, representing active observation and tool use.
Scene 5 background pale lavender: a gently curving three-frame film strip with a pocket watch and memory notebook, representing temporal hierarchy and memory.
Art must be composed clearly in each equal-width region, no important content crossing region boundaries; these regions will be shown separately using CSS positioning. No pseudo-writing, no numbers, no graphs, no logos, no watermark. Deliberate ink geometry, charming editorial illustration, visual rhythm, no 3D or gradients.
```
