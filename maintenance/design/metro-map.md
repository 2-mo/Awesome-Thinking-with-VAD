## Computed layout

`src/components/publication-layout.ts` implements a deterministic, dependency-free staged heuristic:

1. Build the method adjacency graph from shared papers and the configured local reading routes, falling back to chronological paper chains within each method. Include branch-to-parent adjacency and optional `cluster.layoutNear` placement preferences, then minimize weighted squared spans. Actual interchange pairs receive twice the weight of branch edges, keeping shared platforms close; preferred neighbors receive weight four and optional side preferences. Anomaly Criteria & Prompt Optimization stays beside Structured Reasoning & Verification through AnomalyRuler; its earlier explicit `layoutNear` preference is removed. The evaluation branch prefers its actual parent through `layoutNear: reasoning`, putting it above the reasoning trunk while the criteria route stays below. Fork memberships contribute only their parent/branch edge, rather than being counted again as ordinary interchanges. Up to seven methods use an exhaustive ordering search; larger catalogs use bounded adjacent-swap improvement from the catalog order.
2. Pack papers into hidden month groups using compact 72-unit station slots and 44-unit month separation. Independent lines can share an x position; papers on any common line occupy successive slots. A later spacing pass fits constrained columns to names and slopes, then reclaims unused downstream horizontal slack while preserving year-end padding. Order equal-month papers by topology: a transfer that approaches a parent across its branch band precedes that fork, avoiding a crossing of the departing arm. Forks still precede their own same-month branch papers, and other ties use method rank and paper ID. Conflicting transfer preferences yield to the sourced fork order. Reserve enough space after a fork for the first branch arm. The paper identity and date determine the fork; spacing only shapes its outgoing rails. Every earlier month stays to the left of every later month. Quarters follow these packed groups, not proportional calendar widths. Empty quarters retain narrow slots; unknown months remain outside Q1–Q4.
3. Initialize station heights from method order. Ordinary stops start at the method anchor; later packing puts free runs on level shelves, with alternating label positions instead of alternating rail heights. Method anchors follow time-dependent height profiles: earlier alignment uses space before the later generation branch, and sparse criteria segments move closer to reasoning. The profiles are bounded by the available bands so lone-station catalogs remain valid. The current default anchor gap is 336 units, with wider upper corridors and a lower band that opens toward late 2026. Ordinary interchanges average the actual anchor positions, while fork stations retain the parent line anchor. The upper plot margin and year-end padding are compacted without reducing station label sizes. Apply 32 barycentric relaxation passes over neighboring papers, then regularize heights to a 24-unit rail grid. For close ordinary stations already near a 45-degree connection, shift the following level run by at most 24 units from its regularized height to make that diagonal exact. Keep the run level, date coordinates fixed and all shared stations unmoved. Before an upper reading branch begins, ordinary stops on its parent and lower lines may rise by up to 96 units within the long interval after an earlier transfer, leaving at least 80 units before the fork. Shared papers are exempt from this direct lift; barycentric relaxation still follows the neighboring stops. This avoids reserving the full inactive branch band through early years. These heights are an editorial layout choice, with no quantitative vertical-axis meaning.

Reserve planned horizontal and diagonal corridors before placing two-line name/venue labels. Ordinary stations can connect directly; shared stations reserve horizontal approaches and departures, except for the departing branch arm at a fork. Branch arms reserve a direct diagonal departure and open their vertical separation immediately, including sparse and one-stop branches.
